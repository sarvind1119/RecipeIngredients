Best results: Excel (.xlsx) or CSV with columns like Ingredient / Qty / Unit (and optional Dish, Persons). Also Word, PDF, or photo/scan (PNG/JPG/WEBP — not HEIC). One file at a time; maximum 25 MB. Photos can take 30–90 seconds; spreadsheets are usually instant.

Photos and handwritten pages are always sent to Groq vision when the key is set. The AI is never allowed to invent a quantity — illegible amounts stay blank for you to fill in before Save.

Two things the system will always ask you for.
  • How many persons the recipe serves. The documents never state it, and every scaled quantity is worked out from it, so it cannot be guessed.
  • Anything written vaguely - "salt to taste", "2-inch ginger", "50 chopped garlic". These are highlighted on the next screen with the original wording, for you to replace with the quantity the mess actually issues.
