Import recipes from a document

Upload a recipe document from the mess department. The system reads the ingredients out of it and shows you each recipe in the normal recipe form, filled in, for you to check before it is saved. Nothing is saved until you press Save on that screen.

Word (.docx), PDF, Excel, or a scan/photograph. One file at a time; a file holding several recipes is handled a recipe at a time. Maximum 25 MB.

Only the lines that could not be read are sent, and only when the assistant is configured on this PC. It is never allowed to invent a quantity - an unreadable line comes back marked for you either way.

Two things the system will always ask you for.
  • How many persons the recipe serves. The documents never state it, and every scaled quantity is worked out from it, so it cannot be guessed.
  • Anything written vaguely - "salt to taste", "2-inch ginger", "50 chopped garlic". These are highlighted on the next screen with the original wording, for you to replace with the quantity the mess actually issues.
